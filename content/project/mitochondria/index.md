Mitochondrial function integrates genetic, developmental, and environmental influences on performance, and is a likely target of mercury toxicity. In a two-year partial cross-fostering experiment (62 nest pairs, 459 nestlings), I separated natal (genetic and pre-hatching) from rearing (post-hatching) contributions to nestling mitochondrial respiration, and tested whether parental mercury exposure affects mitochondrial function, oxidative stress, and growth. To make this possible in the field, we developed and validated a high-throughput plate-reader method for measuring mitochondrial aerobic metabolism in avian blood cells ([Hadjadji, Ohse et al. 2026, *Biology Open*](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.062712)).

**Collaborators:** Pierre Bize, Blandine Doligez, Antoine Stier, Cloé Hadjadji, Anne Cillard, Mélina Bastianelli
