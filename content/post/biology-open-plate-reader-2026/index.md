Our paper with Cloé Hadjadji, Antoine Stier, and Pierre Bize has been published in *Biology Open*. We show that mitochondrial aerobic metabolism in avian red blood cells can be measured with a general-purpose plate reader, validated against high-resolution respirometry. Unlike dedicated respirometry instruments, which are costly and process few samples at a time, this high-throughput approach makes it feasible to measure mitochondrial function in large numbers of samples from wild birds.

Read the paper: [Hadjadji, Ohse, Stier & Bize (2026), *Biology Open* 15, bio062712](https://doi.org/10.1242/bio.062712).
