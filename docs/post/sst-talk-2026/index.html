Oral presentation at the Swiss Society of Toxicology Annual Meeting 2026

Molly Ohse
November 5, 2026 1 min read Conferences

My abstract, originally accepted as a poster, was selected for an oral presentation at the Swiss Society of Toxicology (SST) Annual Meeting 2026.

Talk: Maternal mercury exposure is associated with lower nestling mitochondrial respiration in a wild songbird: a cross-fostering experiment

The talk presented results from our partial cross-fostering experiment in white-throated dippers, showing that maternal mercury exposure was negatively associated with nestling mitochondrial respiration (OXPHOS), independently of the post-hatching rearing environment.
