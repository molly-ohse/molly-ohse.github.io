Together with Cloé Hadjadji and Pierre Bize, I convened the symposium **Avian mitochondria: Powering ecology, evolution, and conservation** at the 15th European Ornithologists' Union (EOU) Conference in Bangor, UK, on 19 August 2025. The symposium brought together researchers studying mitochondrial function in birds across developmental, dietary, evolutionary, and environmental contexts.

**Talks**
- **Stefania Casagrande:** Mitochondrial biology as a key mechanism linking reduced size and warmer nest temperatures in seabird nestlings
- **Elisa Thoral:** Changes in diet composition affect energy metabolism at several biological scales in Japanese quail
- **Neal Dawson:** Convergent and time-dependent evolutionary changes in mitochondrial function in high-altitude waterfowl
- **Pablo Salmón:** Mitochondrial function and avian migration: lessons from a partial migrant
- **Molly Ohse:** Cascading effects of mercury on mitochondrial traits and individual fitness in a wild population of European dippers
