I will give an oral presentation at the International Ornithological Congress (IOC 2026) in Mérida, Mexico, in the session *Disease and environmental stressors*.

**Talk:** *Maternal mercury concentrations affect offspring mitochondrial function and growth*
**When:** Tuesday 1 December 2026, 16:15
**Where:** Room 26A

Mercury is a pervasive pollutant known to disrupt mitochondrial respiration, yet its effects on developing offspring in the wild are difficult to separate from genetic and environmental influences. In a two-year partial cross-fostering experiment in white-throated dippers (*Cinclus cinclus*), we exchanged nestlings between nests along a gradient of mercury exposure, allowing us to separate pre-hatching (maternal) from post-hatching exposure. The talk presents how maternal mercury relates to nestling mitochondrial respiration, oxidative stress, and growth.

I am grateful to the Swiss Society of Toxicology for a travel grant supporting my attendance.
