Single-population studies cannot tell us how general contaminant effects are. With Cloé Hadjadji and Pierre Bize, I co-led a phylogenetic meta-analysis of experimental and field studies on metals, metalloids, and oxidative stress in birds (55 studies, 609 effect sizes). A second meta-analysis, which began as my undergraduate thesis, uses DDE and avian reproduction as a case study for estimating chemical risk thresholds in the wild.

**Collaborators:** Cloé Hadjadji, Pierre Bize, Francesca Gray, Chinedu Nsude
